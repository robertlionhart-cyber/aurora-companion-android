2. AI output can be wrong
   The companion's replies are generated by AI. They can be inaccurate,

   medical, legal, financial, safety or other important decisions. The

   your device's alarm and battery settings and may be late or missed;
   do not use them for medication or anything critical.

3. Private by design
   The AI runs on your device. There is no account, no cloud service, no ads
   and no telemetry. Internet use: the optional downloads above when you tap
   them, the update check on GitHub (can be turned off), and - only while the
   Online switch is on - the words of a weather, news or look-up question (or
   your city) sent to Open-Meteo, BBC News / Google News, Wikipedia or
   DuckDuckGo. Online results come from those services and can be wrong or
   out of date. Your chats are never sent.

4. Adult mode

5. Your data and your device
   Chats, conversation history, memory notes, characters, reminders and downloads are saved
   in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes
   them. Running AI models uses a lot of processor, memory, storage and
   battery, and can make the device warm. This is beta software, installed
   from outside the Play Store - back up anything important. Use at your own
   risk.
